Who we are

Healthcare shouldn't depend on what you can pay.

Lebanon's economic crisis has put basic healthcare out of reach for many families. HOPE was started by students at the American University of Beirut Faculty of Medicine to help close that gap, one patient at a time.

At our clinic, medical students work alongside licensed physicians to give free consultations, follow-up care and health education. Patients get care, and future doctors learn to practise medicine with compassion and a sense of responsibility to their community.

  • Compassionate, patient-centred care
  • Continuity through follow-up visits
  • Health education that empowers communities

What we offer

Our services

General consultations

Check-ups, diagnosis and treatment of common illnesses by student teams under physician supervision.

Chronic disease care

Ongoing follow-up for conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure, so patients stay on track.

Medication guidance

Help understanding prescriptions and finding affordable ways to get the medication you need.

Health education

Practical advice on nutrition, prevention and healthy habits for patients and their families.

Referrals

When you need more specialised care, we connect you with the right services and specialists.

Screening

Blood pressure, blood sugar and other basic screenings to catch problems early.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes. Consultations at HOPE are completely free of charge. Our mission is to make care available to anyone who can't afford it.

Who will examine me?

You'll be seen by a team of AUB medical students, and a licensed physician supervises and approves every care plan.

Do I need insurance or documents?

No insurance is needed. If you have previous medical reports, test results or a list of medications, please bring them. They help us care for you.

Is this an emergency service?

No. HOPE is not an emergency clinic. In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital emergency department or call the Lebanese Red Cross on 140.

How can I stay updated on clinic days?

Follow @hope.aubfm on Instagram, where we post clinic dates and announcements.
